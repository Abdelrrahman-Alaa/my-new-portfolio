## Product Principles

1. **Business Outcomes First:** Every layout, copy choice, and feature exists to drive customer clarity, trust, and commercial conversion.
2. **Zero-Friction Communication:** Connecting with the developer should be instantaneous; eliminate form fatigue by offering direct WhatsApp and 1-click email copy.
3. **Speed as a Non-Negotiable Standard:** Keep pages sub-second, bundle sizes minimal, and Lighthouse scores at 100/100 across all devices.
4. **Bilingual Parity:** Arabic and English experiences must feel equally native, refined, and culturally resonant, with symmetrical visual balance in RTL and LTR.
5. **Engineering Rigor & Longevity:** Write clean, modular, strictly-typed code that is easy to maintain, scale, or extend.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
